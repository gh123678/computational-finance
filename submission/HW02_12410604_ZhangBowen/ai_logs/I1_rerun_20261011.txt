Claim (c): fixed n, increasing M -- does the distribution become
exactly Gaussian?
======================================================================
  n=5, M=    1,000: sample skewness = +0.8897 (theory +0.8944, approx SE 0.0775)
  n=5, M=   10,000: sample skewness = +0.8835 (theory +0.8944, approx SE 0.0245)
  n=5, M=  100,000: sample skewness = +0.8806 (theory +0.8944, approx SE 0.0077)
  n=5, M=1,000,000: sample skewness = +0.8884 (theory +0.8944, approx SE 0.0024)
Skewness stays near 2/sqrt(n) = 0.894 as M grows: larger M estimates the
TRUE (non-normal) distribution more precisely; it does not make it normal.
Claim (c) is INVALID (also: a bell-shaped histogram is only a diagnostic).
